## Design Context

### Users
Two people: Ben and Ris, a couple in Singapore. They are the only users. They open the app on their phones,
usually on the go: deciding where to eat tonight, logging a meal right after it, or planning a date.
The job is to keep a shared, warm record of places they've eaten together and places they want to try,
and to make the "where should we eat?" decision easy and fun.

### Brand Personality
Warm, playful, intimate. A shared scrapbook more than a review app.
Three words: **cosy, personal, makan**.
Voice: light Singlish, never forced (score words run jialat → shiok; "makan trails", "we went!").
Emotional goal: it should feel like *theirs*, a small private thing made for two, not a product for strangers.

### Aesthetic Direction
- **Palette:** warm paper. Background `#fbf3ea`, paper `#fffaf4`, ink `#3a2a25`, muted `#8a756c`, line `#ecdccd`.
  Each partner has a colour: tomato `#e2572f` (Ben) and rose `#cf5f93` (Ris), with soft tints. Gold `#e9a23b`
  for favourites, green `#4f9a6e` for "been". Tokens live in `app/globals.css`.
- **Type:** serif (Iowan Old Style / Palatino) for headings and names, rounded system sans for UI.
- **Shape:** soft 18px radius, gentle warm shadows. Dark mode supported per device with warm browns, not cold greys.
- **Map:** calm and realistic, like a muted Mapbox style (land `#F3EEE2`, water `#DEE5E3`, park `#E6EAD9`),
  small dot pins (filled = been, ring = want to try), square buttons, boxed labels. **Not cartoony.**
  No toy-world props, emoji textures or MRT lines on the map.
- **Avatars:** the custom chibi SVG avatars of Ben & Ris are the one place for cuteness. Keep them.
- **Anti-references:** generic SaaS dashboards, Yelp/Google review density, toy/game-world maps, heavy gradients.

### Design Principles
1. **Two people, always visible.** Show both scores, both notes, both colours; the "us" is the point.
2. **Calm surfaces, warm details.** Restraint in layout and the map; personality lives in copy, avatars and colour.
3. **Keep it small.** Short lists, small chips (orders max 12), few fields. Add features without adding clutter.
4. **Phone first, one thumb.** Big tap targets, bottom-reachable actions, fast logging after a meal.
5. **Singapore-flavoured.** Local areas, MRT, price bands, hawker culture and Singlish, used lightly.
